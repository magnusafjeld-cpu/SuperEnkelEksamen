# FIE402 Corporate Finance · Mock exam 10

180 minutes · 100 points · 6 exercises · no multiple choice · calculator and one bilingual dictionary permitted. The formula sheet is handed out. Show every calculation.

## Exercise 1: M&A (12 points)

Brekkestø Software ASA, a listed company, has agreed to buy Mørkved Analyse AS, a private data-analytics firm owned by its two founders, for about NOK 2 billion. The board must still decide whether to pay in cash or in newly issued Brekkestø shares.

For each of the four facts below, say whether it points towards paying in cash, in shares or both and explain why. Keep explanations short, 1-2 sentences per fact (3 points each).

1. Brekkestø holds NOK 200 million in cash and its loan covenants allow little new debt.
2. Brekkestø's managers are convinced that the market undervalues Brekkestø's shares.
3. Mørkved's value rests on two large clients. Nobody knows whether the promised synergies will materialise.
4. Both founders want to retire. A cash payment is taxed as a capital gain at once; with shares the tax is deferred until they sell.

## Exercise 2: Governance (12 points)

The board of Skjoldvik Offshore, a listed oil-services company with dispersed ownership, proposes a new bonus plan for its CEO. 60% of the bonus will depend on the three-year total shareholder return and 40% on three targets set by the board: cut carbon emissions per unit of output by 30% within three years, raise the employee-satisfaction score from 62 to 70 and have no serious accidents.

a) Give two distinct arguments why these targets can benefit the shareholders (6 points).

b) Give two distinct ways in which they can harm the shareholders (6 points).

Keep explanations short, 2-3 sentences per argument (3 points each).

## Exercise 3: Security issues (18 points)

Tarevik Bioteknologi AS, a seaweed-biotech firm owned by its founders and a venture fund, goes public. It has 24 million shares and sells 8 million new shares in an IPO at NOK 45 per share. The underwriters charge a fee of 5.3% of the gross proceeds and other expenses are 1.7% of the gross proceeds. On the first day of trading the share closes at NOK 54.

**a) (3 points)** How much does Tarevik raise, net of all costs?

**b) (2 points)** What is Tarevik's market value at the end of the first day?

**c) (5 points)** Assume that the first-day closing price is the fair value. If Tarevik could have sold new shares directly to investors at a fair price, with no fees and no underpricing, raising the same net amount as in a), what would the price per share have been? What did the IPO cost Tarevik's original owners in total?

**d) (4 points)** Many IPOs are like Tarevik's: priced at NOK 45 and worth NOK 54 or NOK 36 at the end of the first day, with equal probability. Informed investors bid only for the good ones. An uninformed investor orders 1,000 shares in every IPO; she receives 250 shares in a good IPO (4 times oversubscribed) and all 1,000 in a bad one. What is her average return?

**e) (4 points)** With the same allocations, at what offer price would uninformed investors just break even? What average first-day return would the IPOs then show?

## Exercise 4: Valuation with leverage (18 points)

Femund Fjernvarme AS, a district-heating company, expects free cash flow of NOK 90 million a year forever, with no growth. Its asset beta is 1.2, the risk-free rate is 3%, the expected market return is 8% and the tax rate is 22%. Femund can borrow at 5% under each of the three debt policies below. Recommended precision is 4 decimal places.

**a) (2 points)** What would Femund be worth with no debt?

**b) (3 points)** Policy 1: Femund keeps its debt at 30% of its market value at all times. Find its levered value with the WACC method.

**c) (3 points)** Policy 2: Femund borrows NOK 300 million today and keeps exactly this debt forever. Find its levered value. Which rate do you discount the tax shields at and why?

**d) (3 points)** Policy 3: Femund sets its interest payments each year equal to 20% of that year's free cash flow. Find its levered value and the debt it holds today.

**e) (4 points)** Under Policy 2, value Femund with the WACC method (find the cost of equity first).

**f) (3 points)** Rank the three policies by value and explain the ranking with the present value of the tax shields per NOK of debt today (2-3 sentences).

## Exercise 5: Options (20 points)

Glimsdal Mineraler ASA has one bond: a zero-coupon bond with face value NOK 1,000 million, due in exactly 2 years. It trades at a yield of 8% a year, while 2-year government zero-coupon bonds yield 3% a year. Rating analysts put the probability that Glimsdal defaults at maturity at 9%; in default the bondholders take over the assets and recover 45% of the face value. A credit default swap (CDS) on the whole issue pays the buyer NOK 550 million if Glimsdal defaults and nothing otherwise; it is paid for today. Use 4 decimal places.

**a) (2 points)** What are Glimsdal's bond and a government zero-coupon bond with the same face value and maturity worth today?

**b) (3 points)** What is the expected annual return on Glimsdal's bond? Why does it differ from the yield?

**c) (3 points)** What risk-neutral probability of default do the bond prices imply? Why does it differ from 9%?

**d) (8 points)** What is the fair price of the CDS? A dealer offers it for NOK 70 million: construct an arbitrage and find the profit.

**e) (4 points)** Glimsdal's assets are worth NOK 1,250 million today. What is its equity worth? Using put-call parity on the firm's assets, explain what would happen to the values of Glimsdal, its equity and its debt if it raised the face value of its debt to buy back shares in a perfect market (2-3 sentences).

## Exercise 6: M&A (20 points)

Tranøy Dagligvare ASA (A) makes a surprise cash offer for Lillesand Bakeri ASA (B): NOK 80 per B share, paid when the deal completes. The deal will complete or fail in exactly 6 months; if it fails, both share prices return to their pre-bid levels. Before the bid, A had 80 million shares at NOK 120 and B had 30 million shares at NOK 64, their stand-alone values. Just after the announcement A trades at NOK 117.00 and B at NOK 76.50. A's management estimates the synergies at NOK 780 million. The risk-free rate is 3% a year. Use 4 decimal places.

**a) (4 points)** If the deal completes, what is its NPV to A's and to B's shareholders by management's estimate (ignore the 6 months in this part)? What is the highest cash price per B share A should offer?

**b) (6 points)** An arbitrageur buys B shares now and holds them until the deal is decided. What is her return over the 6 months if the deal completes and if it fails? What probability of completion does B's price imply?

**c) (6 points)** Now use A's share price with the assumptions of b): in 6 months A trades at its pre-bid price if the deal fails and at its value with the deal if it completes. What synergies S does the market expect? Are A's shareholders better or worse off from the bid?

**d) (4 points)** A pension fund sells its 8% stake in B at NOK 76.50 today instead of waiting for NOK 80. Is this irrational? Who is on the other side of the trade? (3-4 sentences)
